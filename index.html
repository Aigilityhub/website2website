Your questions

AI readiness:
the FAQ

01What is AI readiness?

AI readiness helps organizations prepare their people, processes and policies for successful AI adoption. It answers how ready you are, where the barriers and risks are, and what guardrails you need before you scale.

02Who is the AI Readiness Accelerator for?

It’s designed for SMBs, nonprofits and leadership teams looking for practical AI adoption strategies, whether you’re just starting or trying to bring scattered experiments together.

03Why does human-centred AI matter?

Human-centred AI focuses on people, trust and adoption. The most powerful AI tool in your office isn’t software, it’s your people. Without their trust and buy-in, even the best tools sit unused.

04We already use AI. Is this still for us?

Yes. Many teams use AI in pockets. We help you find additional ways to integrate it into day-to-day work, line it up with your values and put clear guardrails around it.

05How do we get started?

Book a consult. We’ll talk about where you are, what you’re hoping for and which format fits, with no jargon and no pressure.
