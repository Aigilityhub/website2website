Things we believe to be true

AI can do more than ever.

But just because AI can do something doesn’t mean we should hand it over.

AI can draft the recommendation.

Who decides whether it’s right?

AI can summarize the interviews.

Who notices what it missed?

AI can act on your behalf.

Who decides what it’s allowed to do?

AI can make decisions and trigger the next step.

Who remains responsible when it gets something wrong?

The more AI can do, the more important human judgment becomes.
